How this works
  1. Choose a biome or park on the left. The view shows its background exactly as students see it.
  2. Under Already in this background, untick any feature you want to remove, such as a drawn mountain you will replace with a traced one.
  3. Press Add a landmark, then pick a photo (or upload your own). Cut the feature out: Draw a box around it works best.
  4. The cut-out is traced into a few flat shapes and recoloured with this background's own colours. Use the sliders to make it simpler or more detailed, and to choose how closely it follows the scene's colours.
  5. Press Place it. It appears where you are looking. Drag it in the view to move it, scroll over it to resize it (or use the buttons in the corner), or use the sliders. Haze fades distant features like the far hills.
  6. Your work stays in this browser. Download landmarks gives you a .zip to send; it includes the photo credits.
