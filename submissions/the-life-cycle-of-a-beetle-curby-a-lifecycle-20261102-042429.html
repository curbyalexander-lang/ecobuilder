How to explore

  • The stages stand in a circle around you. Follow the arrows from stage 1.
  • Computer: drag to look around, click a stage to read about it, or use Next and Previous.
  • Phone or tablet: move your device or drag to look, tap a stage.
  • VR headset: press the VR button, point and pull the trigger on a stage. A or X goes to the next stage, B or Y to the previous one.
