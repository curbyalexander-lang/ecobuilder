Survival game

How to play

  • Computer: W A S D or the arrow keys to move, drag to look around.
  • Phone or tablet: use the joystick in the corner to move, drag to look.
  • VR headset: press the VR button. Left stick moves, right stick turns.
  • Walk into food to eat it. Keep your energy up and stay away from predators!
