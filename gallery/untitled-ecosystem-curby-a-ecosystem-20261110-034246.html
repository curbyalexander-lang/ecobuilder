How to explore

  • Computer: drag to look around, W A S D or arrow keys to walk, click an organism to read about it.
  • Phone or tablet: move your device or drag to look, tap an organism.
  • VR headset: press the VR button, point and pull the trigger to select, left stick to walk, right stick to turn.
