EcoBuild: Qatar update
======================

Replace these 6 files in the GitHub repo (curbyalexander-lang/ecobuilder). Nothing else changes.

  ecosystem-builder.html
  game-builder.html
  lifecycle-builder.html
  landmark-studio.html
  sizeup.html
  picture-picker.html

What's new
- A "Qatar: national park & nature reserves" group in the environment picker (Ecosystem and Game builders):
  Umm Tais National Park, Khor Al Adaid, Al Thakira, and Ras Abrouq nature reserves.
- Each has a painted 360 backdrop plus 3D features: open water and shorelines, Khor Al Adaid's dunes,
  mangrove trees with pencil roots at Al Thakira and Umm Tais, and mushroom rocks at Ras Abrouq.
- A "Native here" list for each one, with scientific names. New drawings for the grey mangrove, dugong,
  Arabian oryx, sand gazelle, hawksbill turtle, greater flamingo, Socotra cormorant, western reef heron,
  and spiny-tailed lizard. The other species use emoji until you give them art in the Picture Picker.
- Landmark Studio lists the four Qatar settings, so you can trace landmarks from real photos or hide
  any painted or 3D feature.
