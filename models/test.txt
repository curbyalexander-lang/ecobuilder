ECOBUILD STUDENT UPLOADS: SETUP (about 15 minutes, done once)

What you'll end up with
- A "Share your project" button on the EcoBuild home page.
- Students upload their downloaded file and type the class code.
- In review mode (the default), uploads wait in a "submissions" folder that the gallery
  does not show. You get an email, open your review page, preview, and click Approve
  or Remove. Approved projects appear in the gallery about a minute later.
